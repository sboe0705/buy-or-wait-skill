If the price data alone pointed to BUY (e.g. the script's pre-assessment verdict was BUY) but the final verdict is WAIT or DON'T BUY because of news or an upcoming event, the answer explicitly tells the person that there is a buy signal on the chart that is being held back, names the reason and says when to re-check. If the price data did not point to BUY, or the final verdict is BUY, this criterion is met.
